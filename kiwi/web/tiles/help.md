This page tries the calibration with the letter tiles, the way the demo uses them.

## Where the tiles go

Two bands round the arm, drawn on the grid and over the camera's picture, show where the tiles go:

- **Pickup area**, green: the outer band, further from the arm. Set the tiles down here, letters up, a little
  apart from each other.
- **Dropoff area**, blue: the inner band, close to the arm. Keep it clear: the arm sets the words down here, along
  the dotted arc in front of the arm.

A tile anywhere else is not picked up.

## Trying it

1. Set the tiles down in the pickup area.
2. **LOCATE TILES**. The arm moves out of the way, the camera finds the tiles and reads their letters. A tile's
   color says whether the arm can pick it up.
3. Click a tile to bring the cup over it: the cup should sit centered over the tile.
4. Type letters of the tiles ready to pick up, then **PLACE**. The arm sets them down on the arc in the dropoff
   area, in order, each the right way up for the reader.

Put the tiles back in the pickup area before the next LOCATE TILES.

## Settings

- **tile height**: how thick the tiles are.
- **tile offset**: how far the cup presses into a tile to grip it.
- **reader**: where the person reading the word stands.

The demo uses the same settings.
