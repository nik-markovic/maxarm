Fine Tune makes the camera calibration better, point by point, with the red cube, and lets you check it. The
camera page's three cubes are its first points, 1 to 3.

## Add points

1. Take the blue and green cubes away.
2. Set the red cube down, a corner pointing at the camera.
3. Click the grid where it stands, or **FIND RED CUBE**: the cup comes down just over it.
4. Center the cube under the cup with the arrows. Watch the cube, not the screen.
5. **RECORD POINT**. The arm moves away and the camera looks at the cube. A close-up shows what it found:
   **KEEP** it, **REDO** to look again, or **UNDO**.
6. Again in a new place each time, near the arm and far out, left and right. The dashed circle shows a good
   place for the next one.
7. **SAVE CALIBRATION**. New points are used only once saved.

## The points

- **off now**: how far the cube the camera sees is from where the arm said the cup was. 0 once saved.
- **without it**: the same, as if the point were left out: how well the calibration does between points.
- A red number: the point does not agree with the others. The cube was moved, or the cup was off its center.
  SAVE leaves it out: remove it (×) and record it again.

## Find the places that need a point

**ARM TO DESK** and **FIND RED CUBE** show how well the calibration does. Their **?** says how. Where it misses,
record a point nearby.

For more, with pictures, see CALIBRATION.md in the demo's repository.
