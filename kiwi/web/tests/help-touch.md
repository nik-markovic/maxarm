Click **ARM TO DESK**, then a point of the desk in the picture. The cup comes down onto the desk there: the desk
itself, so anything standing there is pushed aside.

Once the arm is down, a green circle is drawn where the arm says the cup is.
**The green circle should sit on the cup in the picture.** Where it does not, the calibration is off there: record
a Fine Tune point near that place.

Try it all over the desk to find the places that need a point.
