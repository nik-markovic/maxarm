Set the red cube down anywhere in view, a corner pointing at the camera, then **FIND RED CUBE**. The arm moves out
of the way, the camera finds the cube, and the cup comes down just over it.

How far the cup ends up from the cube's center is the calibration's miss and the arm's own together. So this is
mostly a quick way to bring the cup to the next point: center the cube under the cup, then **RECORD POINT**.

On the cube's top: a dot where the arm was sent, and a green ring where the arm then said the cup was.
**ARM TO DESK** shows the same, larger, to look at closely: blue where the arm was sent, green where it landed,
until you click the desk there.
