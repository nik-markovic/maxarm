This page is for getting to know the arm: move it by hand and see what it reports.

- Click the grid: the cup goes to that point, at the height beside the grid.
- The height slider, **Z+** and **Z−** move the cup up and down. **SAFE** lifts it clear of everything.
  **▼ 2 mm** lets the slider go lower, **▲ 20 mm** higher.
- The arrows nudge the cup sideways. **PRECISE** lands it exactly on the target, slowly.
- **Cup** turns the suction cup. **Suction** switches the pump on; then the page says whether the cup sealed.
- **Rotate view** turns the grid to match where you stand.
- **RESET**, top right, stops the arm at any time, lets go and sends it home.

## The desk

The arm knows where a flat desk is, as long as the suction cups under its base hold it pressed flat on the desk.
To check, lower the cup onto the desk in a few places, near and far, with **Suction** on: it should seal with
"Cup above the desk" near 0 everywhere.

For more, with pictures, see CALIBRATION.md in the demo's repository.
