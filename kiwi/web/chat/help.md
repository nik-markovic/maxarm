Visitors ask the language model on the board's Ara240 for a word made of the tiles, and the arm sets it down.

1. **LOCATE TILES**, so the tiles' letters are known.
2. Ask, or click a suggestion: **go** for the longest word, **a 5-letter word**, or a hint like
   **a farm animal**. **spell** a word of your own.
3. Add **don't place it** to see the word first, then say **place it**.
4. **NEW VISITOR** starts the conversation again.

**STOP** cuts an answer short; **RESET**, top right, stops the arm. Put the tiles back before the next word: after a
word has been set down, the next request looks at the tiles again first.

The model needs a few minutes to load after the board starts: the pill at the top of the chat says when it is
ready. Under each answer, **what the model did** shows what went to and from the model.
